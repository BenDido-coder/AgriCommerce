Getting Started

What is AgriCommerce?

AgriCommerce is an Ethiopian online agricultural marketplace that connects farmers, suppliers, and buyers. It supports direct sales, logistics coordination, and local payment options.

Who can use this platform?

Farmers, wholesalers, retailers, suppliers, and individual buyers within Ethiopia. Only a valid phone number (preferably with WhatsApp or Telegram) is required to join.

Is the site available in Amharic?

Yes. The platform supports both English and Amharic. Language can be changed from the top menu or settings page.

Transactions & Listings

How do I list my agricultural products?

After signing up, go to your dashboard → click “Post a Product” → fill out the form and upload photos. Listings will be reviewed and published shortly.

What kind of products can I sell?

Permitted categories include: crops (teff, maize, sorghum), livestock, fertilizers, seeds, tools, and dried foods. Expired or counterfeit items are prohibited and will be removed.

Can I promote my listings?

Currently, all listings are free. In the future, we may offer paid “featured” listings or homepage banners for extra visibility.

How are payments handled?

Buyers and sellers agree on payment outside the platform using options like Telebirr, CBE Birr, HelloCash, or bank transfers. We also support escrow-style payments with admin verification for added security.

Can I buy from sellers in other regions?

Yes. AgriCommerce works nationwide. You can buy or sell across any Ethiopian region. Arrange transport through listed logistics providers or direct agreement.

Trust & Safety

How do I know a seller or buyer is real?

We verify phone numbers, review listings, and monitor user behavior. Check their product photos, ask questions, and confirm identity before payment. Trust your instincts.

What should I do if something goes wrong?

If a trade fails or you're scammed, report the listing or user through the “Report” button or contact us via WhatsApp. We'll investigate and take necessary action.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use AES-256 encryption and store data on secure cloud servers in Africa. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Technical Help

What devices does the platform support?

You can access AgriCommerce on any device—mobile phone, tablet, or desktop—with a modern browser like Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click “Forgot Password” on the login page and follow the steps. If you can’t access your email or phone, contact our support team for help.
