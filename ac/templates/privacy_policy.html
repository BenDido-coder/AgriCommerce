Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 8, 2025

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AgriCommerce collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your personal information when you use our website and services. It complies with the Ethiopian Personal Data Protection Proclamation (Draft 2021), GDPR (EU), and CCPA (California).

2. Data We Collect (GDPR Article 4)

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email, profile photo, address.
  • Transaction Data: Product listings, order history, payment status.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, location, device ID, access time.
  • Cookies and Tracking: Session cookies, preferences, analytics, and referrer URLs.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process orders and payments via M-Pesa or bank transfer.
  • To manage user accounts and provide logistics support.
  • To personalize content and product recommendations.
  • To communicate system updates, promotions, and help requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and resolve fraud or security issues.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We only collect and process your data where:

  • You have given consent.
  • Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract (e.g., buying/selling).
  • It is required for compliance with local laws or a legal obligation.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share data with:

  • Logistics partners to enable delivery of products.
  • Payment providers for transaction processing and verification.
  • Regulatory authorities upon legal request (e.g., Ethiopian Customs or NBE).
  • Technology partners (e.g., AWS, analytics services) under strict data processing agreements.

6. Data Storage and Security

All personal data is encrypted using AES-256 and stored on AWS Africa (Cape Town) region servers. We follow OWASP Top 10 security guidelines and regularly audit access controls.

Access to personal data is restricted to authorized admin staff only.

7. International Transfers

We do not store data outside Africa unless required for processing (e.g., analytics). Any such transfers comply with GDPR-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

8. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

  • Right to access – You can request a copy of your stored data.
  • Right to rectification – You can correct inaccuracies in your personal data.
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten) – You can request deletion of your account and related data.
  • Right to data portability – You may export your data in a readable format.
  • Right to object – You may object to marketing or profiling activities.

9. Data Retention

We retain user data as long as the account is active or required for tax, legal, or audit purposes. Inactive accounts may be anonymized after 12 months of inactivity.

10. Cookies Policy

We use cookies to remember your preferences, improve security, and analyze platform usage. You can manage cookie settings in your browser or opt out of non-essential cookies.

11. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach, affected users will be notified within 72 hours as per GDPR Article 33. We will take immediate action to isolate and fix the vulnerability.

12. Updates to Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated for legal or operational reasons. You will be notified via dashboard or email. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.
